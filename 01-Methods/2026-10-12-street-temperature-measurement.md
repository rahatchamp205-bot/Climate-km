We used low-cost temperature loggers placed at 1.5 m height on
selected streets in Mirpur and Mohammadpur between 28 Sep and
8 Oct 2026.

Key decisions:
- Logging interval: 15 minutes
- Devices shielded from direct sun
- Calibration checked against BMD station data
