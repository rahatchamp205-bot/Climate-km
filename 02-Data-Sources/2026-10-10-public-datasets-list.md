- Bangladesh Meteorological Department (BMD): daily station
  temperature and rainfall. We use it to calibrate our loggers.
- Flood Forecasting and Warning Centre (FFWC): river levels and
  flood bulletins during monsoon.
- OpenStreetMap: street and building maps for choosing logger sites.
- Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics (BBS): population census data.
